# A picture book directory for the overwhelmed parent at the public library

It’s risky business walking into the public library unprepared. Finding picture books worth your while can be a challenge especially with small children in tow creating a frenzied rather than leisurely browsing experience. So to help a fellow overwhelmed parent out and make for easy shelf scanning, I’ve created a list of books my kids and I have enjoyed.

The following range from the good to the extra ’specially good. They are books I find funny or beautiful or wise, and the best are all three at once. You might not share my taste for all of them. You might disagree with the parenting style in Harriet, You’ll Drive Me Wild! or the fact that a child is depicted nude in The Night Kitchen or that Drummer Hoff has the ending it does, but I guarantee that some books on here will light up yours and your children’s reading lives (or that of your grandchildren, nieces and nephews, godchildren, or friend’s borrowed children).

For the most part these are books that appear in the picture book section of the library. I’ve avoided nursery rhyme and poetry collections, early readers, non-fiction, and board books (although I’ve included some board books that are also printed as picture books). At our local library, some fairy tales and folk tales appear in the picture book section and some appear in the non-fiction. I’ve included those all here.

I will update this list with new titles based on my findings at our weekly library trips, so bookmark this post on the webpage and check back in.

And finally, if you really, really love a book and want it in your home library, it would make my day if you bought it through one of my affiliate links.
